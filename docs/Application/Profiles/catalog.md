# Catalog and publishing

The **Profile Catalog** is a library of ready profiles: official profiles for LEGO® sets made by the MOCPilot team and community profiles shared by other builders. It is the same catalog as on [mocpilot.com](https://mocpilot.com).

## Open the catalog

- Phone and tablet: **Search Online** on the profiles page (phone: **+** → **Search Online**).
- Computer: **File → Search Online**, or **Profile Catalog**.

The catalog needs an internet connection. If it cannot be shown inside the app on your device, use **Open in browser**.

The catalog has **Official** and **Community** sections and a search by name, description, hashtags, author and set number. Cards show how often a profile was downloaded.

## Install a profile

Open a profile in the catalog and add it. A copy appears in your profile list.

- While you don't change it, the profile runs exactly as published, including paid themes you don't own.
- When the author publishes a new version, the card shows **Update available**. Tap **Update** to install it; nothing changes until you do.
- The first time you change it, MOCPilot warns you: after the change the profile no longer receives catalog updates, and products you don't own are replaced by the Basic theme or locked. See [Manage profiles](/docs/Application/Profiles/ManageProfiles#paid-themes-in-shared-profiles).

On the website you can also press **Add to account** on any catalog profile; it then arrives in the app through [sync](/docs/Application/Account/Sync).

## Publish your profile

Share your model with everyone:

1. Make sure you are signed in and the profile is synced (the catalog copies it from your account; you are shown as the author).
2. Open **Edit profile** → **Publish to the catalog** (on a computer also **Publish Profile**).
3. Check the requirements, fill in the form and press **Send for review**.

### Requirements

Before sending for review, the profile needs:

- a name of at least 3 characters, not a default one like "New profile" or "Untitled";
- a picture;
- a description of at least 20 characters;
- at least one hub;
- something to control: a dashboard controller or the gamepad;
- a program with at least one block or row;
- at least one hashtag.

The publish page shows what is missing.

### The form

- **Description**: what the model does and how to control it.
- **Visibility**: **Public** (shown in the catalog) or **By link only**.
- **Hubs**: taken from the profile.
- Additional fields such as set number may be asked.
- **What's new**: for a new version.

### Review

A moderator checks every submission. The result arrives in **Notifications** (the bell):

- "Your profile … passed review": it is now in the catalog on the website and in the app.
- "Your profile … did not pass review": the moderator's note says what to fix; fix it and send it again.

The publish page shows the status: waiting for review, published, published with a new version waiting for review, rejected or hidden.

You can send up to 5 profiles or new versions a month; the page shows how many are left.

### New versions and unpublishing

Publishing the same profile again sends a **new version** for review. People who installed it see **Update available**. You can also hide a published profile.
