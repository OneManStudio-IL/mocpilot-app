# Layered themes

The paid themes use the **layered** format. A layered control is drawn from several stacked layers instead of one picture:

- **image** layers: the body, bezel, glass and shadows, prepared with their effects;
- **shapes and arcs**: rings, scales and progress arcs that move with the value;
- **text and icons**: labels, numbers and the icon you choose;
- **monitor text**: the text a monitor shows.

The app combines the layers live: the handle of a slider moves over its track, a speedometer needle turns, an arc fills, the accent colour tints the right layers.

## What this gives you

- **Sharp at every size**: S, M and L and any screen density.
- **Your colours**: the accent colour and theme options change the look without new graphics.
- **Smooth and light**: a few images per control, so dashboards stay fast even on older phones.
- **Same behaviour**: a joystick of any theme behaves exactly like a Basic joystick, so you can change themes without touching the program.

## Versions and compatibility

Themes are updated on the server from time to time: new controls, fixes, better graphics. The app downloads the newest version that it can show. An older app keeps getting the version made for it, so a theme never breaks after an update. To get the newest look, keep MOCPilot up to date.
