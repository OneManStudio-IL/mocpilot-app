# MocPilot Pro and purchases

MOCPilot is free to use: both editors, all hubs, the Basic theme and 2 hub slots per profile. **MocPilot Pro** opens everything else, or you can buy single items.

## What's free

- Logic Blocks and Quick Links, all supported hubs, motors and sensors;
- the **Basic** theme with all its controls;
- 2 hub slots per profile;
- sync, the catalog, publishing, the web editor.

## MocPilot Pro

"Everything in MOCPilot, on all your devices":

- **All themes**: every dashboard theme and style, including future ones;
- **All extensions**: steering wheel, pedals, extensions and all hub slots;
- **Everything for free**: nothing to buy separately;
- **Remote control**: run a profile on another phone, tablet or computer and control it from your device or the [web editor](/docs/Introducing/WebEditor#run-a-profile);
- **AI builder**: build and change profiles by describing them (see [AI builder](/docs/Application/BlockBuilder/Editor#ai-builder));
- **More with time**: new Pro features as they are released.

### Plans

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Monthly | $4.99 per month |
| Yearly ("Best value") | $41.99 per year |

Prices may differ by country and store. New subscribers get a **7-day free trial**.

MocPilot Pro is linked to your MOCPilot account, so it works on all your devices; you need to be signed in to subscribe. In the browser you cannot subscribe; subscribe in the app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android or Windows and it works in the web editor too.

### Manage or cancel

**Account → MocPilot Pro → Manage subscription** opens the store where you subscribed (App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store). If it was bought on another platform, manage it there. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

The page shows the state: active and when it renews, active until a date with auto-renewal off, the free trial end, or a payment problem to fix in the store.

## Purchases

Single items you buy are yours forever, even without Pro:

| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Themes: Racing, Building machinery, Hi-Fi Audio, Aura | all controls of the theme |
| Steering wheel | three styles |
| Pedals | three styles |
| Additional hub slot (×3) | one more hub slot in every profile |

Buy themes and controls in the dashboard's **Add Controller** palette ([how](/docs/Application/Themes/GettingThemes)), hub slots with **Unlock Slot** in My Hubs.

## Restore purchases

On a new device or after reinstalling: sign in with the same account, open **Account → Purchases** and tap **Restore**. The same for Pro on the subscription page.

Messages you may see:

- **Subscription linked to another account**: this store account already has MocPilot Pro for another MOCPilot account. Sign in with that account.
- **Nothing to restore**: no active subscription was found for this store account.
- **Almost done**: the purchase went through but the server could not be reached; it is completed automatically when you are online.

**Purchases settings** (Use alternative store, Open in the system browser) are for regions where the usual stores are not available.
