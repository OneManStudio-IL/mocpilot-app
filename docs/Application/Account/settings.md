# Settings

Phone: **Settings** in the bottom bar. Tablet: **Settings** in the side bar. Computer: the gear button in the top bar (⌘, / Ctrl+,).

## Account

The top of Settings shows your account, or **Create an account or Sign In** in guest mode. See [Account](/docs/Application/Account/UserAccount).

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sound** | sounds of the app and of controls (steering wheel and pedal sound effects) |
| **Vibration** | a short vibration when you touch controls (devices with vibration) |
| **Keep screen on** | prevents the screen from turning off while a profile is open; turn it on for driving |
| **Connect a Remote Controller** | phone and tablet: let another device or the web editor control the profiles on this device (scan its code). See [Web editor](/docs/Introducing/WebEditor#run-a-profile) |
| **Language** | the app language; choose and tap **Apply** |
| **Dark mode** | dark or light appearance |

On a computer, remote control is connected with the **QR** button in the top bar.

## About

**Version** opens **About MOCPilot** with the Privacy Policy, Terms, release notes and licenses.

## Profile settings

Settings that belong to one profile are in the editor:

- **Editor Settings**: block level, launch block by clicking, stop hub ports when program stops; see [Working in the editor](/docs/Application/BlockBuilder/Editor#editor-settings);
- **Dashboard Settings**: background and grid; see [Dashboard editor](/docs/Application/Dashboard/DashboardOverview#dashboard-settings);
- **Gamepad**: see [Gamepad](/docs/Application/Dashboard/Gamepad).

With **Settings** sync on, app settings are the same on all your devices. See [Sync](/docs/Application/Account/Sync).
