# Account

A MOCPilot account keeps your profiles, settings and purchases safe and available on all your devices.

## Why an account

- Profiles and settings are stored on the server and synced across your devices.
- Restore everything if you change or lose a device.
- Purchases and MocPilot Pro work on all your devices and in the web editor.
- Publish profiles to the catalog and get notifications.

## Sign in

On the sign-in screen use **Email or Login** and **Password**, or **Sign In with Google** / **Sign In with Apple ID**.

## Create an account

**Create an account**: enter a username, your email twice and a password, or **Continue with Google** / **Continue with Apple ID**. Confirm your email with the activation mail; some features are disabled until the account is verified (**Resend activation mail** if it did not arrive).

## Forgot the password

Tap **Reset** next to "Forgot password?", enter your email, and type the code from the email to set a new password.

## Guest mode

**Continue without an account** lets you use the app right away. Everything stays only on this device and is lost if the app is removed or the device is lost. You can sign in later in **Settings → Account**.

## Account page

Phone: **Settings → Account**. Tablet: **Account** in the side bar. Computer: the **Account** button in the top bar.

- **Account details**: login, email, region and account ID (tap an item to copy it).
- **Cloud sync**: see [Sync between devices](/docs/Application/Account/Sync).
- **Purchases**: see [MocPilot Pro and purchases](/docs/Application/Account/Pro#purchases).
- **Change password**.
- **Sign Out**.
- **Delete account**.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** permanently deletes your account data: personal information, preferences, history and profiles on the server. You also lose access to purchased and subscribed content linked to the account. This cannot be undone.

Enter your current password and confirm with **Slide to delete account** (touch) or **Hold to delete account** (computer).

An active subscription is managed by the store: cancel it there before deleting the account.

## Notifications

The **bell** opens **Notifications**: results of the profiles you publish, replies to your support requests and news from the MOCPilot team. You can mark them read, select and delete them.
