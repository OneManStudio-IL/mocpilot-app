# Frequently asked questions

### Is MOCPilot free?
Yes. Both editors, all hubs, the Basic theme and 2 hub slots per profile are free. Paid themes, steering wheel, pedals, extra hub slots, remote control and the AI builder are available as purchases or with [MocPilot Pro](/docs/Application/Account/Pro).

### Which hubs are supported?
LEGO® Technic Hub, Technic Move Hub, City Hub, BOOST Move Hub, WeDo 2.0, Robot Inventor, the Powered Up remote, BuWizz 2.0 and 3.0 Pro, SBrick and SBrick Plus, Mould King 4.0 and 6.0. See [Supported devices](/docs/Introducing/SupportedDevices).

### Quick Links or Logic Blocks?
Quick Links for "this control moves that motor", ready in minutes with no code. Logic Blocks for programs with logic: sequences, conditions, variables, sensors. See the [comparison](/docs/Application/Profiles/CreateProfile#choose-the-program-type).

### Can I change the program type of a profile?
No, it is chosen when the profile is created. Create a new profile with the other type.

### I used Controlz. Is Quick Links the same?
Quick Links follows the same idea, rows from a control to a hub, and adds processing blocks, hub sensors as inputs, live values, undo, and no row limit. See [Quick Links](/docs/Application/Links/LinksIntroducing).

### Where are the prebuilt profiles for LEGO sets?
In the [Profile Catalog](/docs/Application/Profiles/Catalog) (**Search Online**): official profiles for sets, plus community profiles.

### Can I use several hubs in one model?
Yes, up to 5 per profile: 2 free slots and 3 that can be unlocked.

### Does it work with a gamepad?
Yes, Xbox and PlayStation style gamepads supported by your device. See [Gamepad](/docs/Application/Dashboard/Gamepad).

### Can I use MOCPilot on a computer?
Yes, on Mac and Windows, and in the browser on mocpilot.com for editing. See [Phone, tablet and desktop](/docs/Introducing/Layouts) and [Web editor](/docs/Introducing/WebEditor).

### Can I drive a model from the browser?
The browser cannot use Bluetooth hubs. With MocPilot Pro, your phone connects the hubs and the browser becomes the remote. See [Web editor](/docs/Introducing/WebEditor#run-a-profile).

### Do I need an account?
No, but without one your profiles live only on one device and are lost with the app. An account gives sync, backup, purchases on all devices and publishing.

### How do I share a profile with a friend?
**Share…** creates a `.dat` file; your friend uses **Import Profile…**. Or publish it to the [catalog](/docs/Application/Profiles/Catalog).

### My friend's profile has controls I don't own. What happens?
You can run it as it is. If you edit it, controls of themes you don't own switch to the Basic theme and special controls are locked until purchased.

### Can I make my own themes?
Not in the app. Themes are made by the MOCPilot team; you can configure controls and save your designs. See [Create controls from a theme](/docs/Application/Themes/CreatingControls).

### What should I write in an AI builder request?
Name the hub, say what is connected to each port (drive motor, steering motor, lights, sensor), what each one should do and how you want to control it. Without this the AI has to guess the ports and roles, and the result may need fixing. See [How to get a good result](/docs/Application/BlockBuilder/Editor#ai-builder-tips).

### Is MOCPilot made by LEGO?
No. MOCPilot is an independent app. LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor or endorse MOCPilot.

### How do I contact support?
**Help → Ask a question** or **Report a bug** in the app. Community: the MOCPilot Telegram group (link in **Help**).
